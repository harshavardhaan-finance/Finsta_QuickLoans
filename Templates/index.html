My Project Journey

I built Finsta QuickLoans as my personal fintech project to bring together finance, credit-risk analysis and machine learning in a simple quick-loan experience. The project uses a historical loan dataset to train a credit-risk model, then combines the model's Probability of Default with an affordability and pricing policy to produce an indicative offer.
